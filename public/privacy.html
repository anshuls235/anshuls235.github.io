A SMALLER DATA FOOTPRINT

Privacy, by design.

This portfolio uses minimal, optional first-party analytics to understand which work visitors find useful. Analytics runs only when configured; the site works without it.

What may be collected

Page and project views; project, profile, CV and contact link clicks; time of the event; the page path without its query string; referring domain; and simple campaign labels (UTM source, medium and campaign). Email contents, search queries, contact details and full referring URLs are never sent by the analytics client.

A random session marker lives only in this page’s memory for up to 30 minutes. It is not saved in cookies or browser storage. Reloading a page starts a new marker, so session counts are approximate, not a count of unique people.

What is left out

No advertising cookies, persistent visitor identifiers, fingerprints, precise location, or named visitor profiles. This application does not store raw or hashed IP addresses or user-agent strings. The hosting and database providers necessarily receive network requests and may retain operational logs under their own policies.

Your choices

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable analytics requests. Blocking JavaScript or the analytics endpoint also prevents tracking and does not prevent reading this site. A theme preference, if selected, is saved in your browser only and is never sent to analytics.

Storage and access

Analytics is sent to a Supabase Edge Function and stored in private database tables. It is available only to the site owner for portfolio reporting. Analytics is retained for historical portfolio reporting. Automatic deletion is currently disabled. No analytics is sold or made publicly readable. Client-side measurement cannot guarantee that every visit is counted.

External links

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Medium, Kaggle and other sites open services with their own privacy practices. No embedded third-party social widgets, tracking pixels, remote fonts or video players are used here.
